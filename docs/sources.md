# Sources and editorial scope

**Purpose.** Make it possible for a reader to distinguish illustrative career guidance from claims that can be checked against a publisher.

**Summary.**

- Reviewed on 24 November 2026 against the primary sources below.
- Company publications support reading exercises, not claims of verified corporate success.
- Provider pages determine current course names, content, prices and requirements.
- Removed unsupported market statistics, salary bands, hiring-growth figures and quotations.

## Editorial decisions

The original guide mixed fictional workdays with numerical market and company claims without citations. The showcase update separates those categories. The company cards now invite critical reading, rather than presenting attributed outcomes as independently established facts. The fictional downgrade no longer names a real company.

Career progression and skills are teaching aids, not statistical rankings. Salaries and course prices were removed because the repository did not establish a source, market, date and methodology for them. The anonymous consultant quotation was removed because its attribution could not be established.

Review links and provider names before promoting the guide again. Do not add a salary, legal deadline or market figure without a source, date, scope and explanation of its limits.
